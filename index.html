01 · CONTEXT

It started with one week, one library and one file that grew too big.

In late June 2026 I wanted a lo-fi tool I could bend to my own way of working. I built the first Solide on GrapesJS in a week, 84 commits from June 26 to July 2, and it worked. Then I audited it: one engine file of 1,179 lines, changed 52 times in 30 days. Every new feature had to squeeze through that one door.

On July 3 I started over. This time the framing, the flows and the UI system came first, and the code came after. That second start is the Solide you see here, and I have since drawn more than 300 Tasmi' screens on it, from rough lo-fi to final hi-fi.

84COMMITS IN WEEK ONEGrapesJS, June 26 to July 2. Then the audit found one engine file of 1,179 lines.
02 · USER PROBLEM

Three tools, two AI bills, and a gap only my eyes were checking.

A normal day meant laying out screens in one app, drawing icons in another, and paying for the AI that plugs into each. Every switch cost a little focus, and every plan a little money.

Then came the handoff. Porting Solide's own sidebar from its mockup took four rounds of me spotting mismatches by eye. When I finally automated the check, its first run on a section I had already reviewed three times found 134 mismatches. My eyes were the only test, and they were losing.

06 · SPECS AND PROOF

Every behaviour is drawn and signed off before its code.

The pen drew corners only. A curve could arrive in an SVG file, but dragging one of its points bent it out of shape, and a circle showed 5 points instead of 4.

So curves became 24 behaviours, A to X, each one drawn on the canvas and approved over four review rounds before I wrote a line of code. Six of them below.
